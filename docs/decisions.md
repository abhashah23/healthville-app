# Decisions and open questions

Plain-language record of what has been decided, and what is still open.
Newest updates at the top of each section.

## Decided (from Abha, Oct 13, 2026)

- Pilot group starts Thursday Nov 5, 2026. Phase 1 is aimed at this date.
- Healthville uses Microsoft 365. The recommended build is a small custom
  web app hosted in Microsoft's Canadian data centre (Toronto), pending
  approval.
- Facilitators see participant names. Participants are also assigned a code.
- Admins: Abha, Rohit, Doris. About 10 participants in the pilot.
- Week 1 starts on the Thursday of Session 1.
- A week cannot be submitted late, and cannot be changed after it is
  submitted.
- A week cannot be submitted with any time of day left empty. "What I was
  doing" is required.
- Participants can see their own past weeks and a chart of their scores.
- English only.
- Dr. Howell approves all participant-facing wording.

## Needs approval before real participants use it

- Hosting provider and region (including whether a US-owned provider with a
  Canadian data centre is acceptable).
- Which privacy laws apply to participants in Canada, the US, Europe and
  Australia, and the consent wording shown before the first entry.
- Privacy Notice updated to describe this app.
- Data agreements with the hosting and email providers.
- Retention period and deletion process.
- Breach response: what happens and who is told.
- Before Phase 3: permission to use the FACIT Fatigue Scale in an app, and
  whether the Pre/Post Survey needs research ethics approval.
