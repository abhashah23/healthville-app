# Decisions and open questions

Plain-language record of what has been decided, and what is still open.
Newest updates at the top of each section.

## Decided (from Abha, Oct 13, 2026)

- Pilot group starts Thursday Nov 5, 2026. Phase 1 is aimed at this date.
- Healthville uses Microsoft 365. The recommended build is a small custom
  web app hosted in Microsoft's Canadian data centre (Toronto), pending
  approval.
- Facilitators see participant names. Participants are also assigned a code.
- Admins: Abha, Rohit, Doris. About 10 participants in the pilot.
- Week 1 starts on the Thursday of Session 1.
- A week cannot be submitted late, and cannot be changed after it is
  submitted.
- A week cannot be submitted with any time of day left empty. "What I was
  doing" is required.
- Participants can see their own past weeks and a chart of their scores.
- English only.
- Dr. Howell approves all participant-facing wording.

## Decided (from Abha, second round)

- Fatigue score is 0 to 10.
- A week closes at the start of the next session (Thursday 1:00 PM ET).
- Staff can see what was entered in a week that closed without being
  submitted. It is marked "Not submitted".
- The three weekly reflection questions are required.
- Dr. Howell is an admin.
- There are 14 facilitators.
- The participant code is shown in the app, next to the name.
- LMS: MyNuclo (https://healthville.mynuclo.com/). Phase 1 is a link from it.
- Colours and fonts follow the brief: teal #028090 headings, Poppins.

## Decided (Energy Balance Blueprint, from Abha)

- Added as an extra tracker, from the "Energy Balance Blueprint Worksheet
  (day at a glance)".
- The two "Activity" columns on the paper sheet mean the same thing. In the
  app, each activity is its own line with 1 to 4 points.
- Filled in on any days the participant chooses, during the week after
  Session 2.
- The app shows a daily total: activity points minus replenishing points.
- Same rules as the weekly tracker: submit once, no changes after, closes at
  the start of Session 3, staff see it once submitted or closed.

## From the handouts (Weekly Fatigue Tracker, My Spoons)

- Energy Balance points are spoons an activity costs. The app shows the
  examples from the "My Spoons" handout: watching a movie 1, bathing 2,
  cooking food 3, going to work or school 4.
- The weekly tracker label now matches the handout: "What I was doing".

## Choices made while building (Abha can change these)

- Facilitators do not see a week while it is still in progress. They see it
  once it is submitted or closed.
- Averages and charts use submitted weeks only.
- Severity bands are shown for single scores, not for averages, because the
  handout bands are whole numbers only.
- Participants can fill in any day of the open week, in any order.
- Participants see the band key from the handout under the score buttons.
- Energy Balance: comments are per line (the paper sheet has a comments
  column per row). Up to 40 activities and 20 replenishing activities a day,
  matching the 20 rows on the paper sheet.
- Energy Balance: it can be submitted once at least one activity is entered
  on any day.

## Open questions

- Energy Balance: the paper sheet says replenishing can "add a spoon". The
  app treats replenishing points as taken off the day's total. Is that right?

- Week 6 has no next session. For now it closes one week after Session 6, at
  1:00 PM ET. Is that right?
- Words for each end of the 0 to 10 scale (for example "0 = no fatigue,
  10 = worst possible"). For now only the numbers are shown.
- Should group and participant averages show a severity band? If so, how
  should averages like 3.5 be rounded?
- Which of the 14 facilitators run the pilot group, and is it one group?

## Needs approval before real participants use it

- Hosting provider and region (including whether a US-owned provider with a
  Canadian data centre is acceptable).
- Which privacy laws apply to participants in Canada, the US, Europe and
  Australia, and the consent wording shown before the first entry.
- Privacy Notice updated to describe this app.
- Data agreements with the hosting and email providers.
- Retention period and deletion process.
- Breach response: what happens and who is told.
- Before Phase 3: permission to use the FACIT Fatigue Scale in an app, and
  whether the Pre/Post Survey needs research ethics approval.
